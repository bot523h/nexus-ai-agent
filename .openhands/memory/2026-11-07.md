## Independent audit of F1 (same session) — 1 real fix
- Full audit of the F1 diff (code, migration, tests, docs, CI head pins, golden).
  Verdict: sound, but found ONE real narrow correctness gap and fixed it:
  `_reconcile_existing` advanced the fencing token with a read-modify-write
  (`if attempt > row.attempt: row.attempt = attempt`). Two concurrent re-binds of
  the same artifact (successor at a higher token racing a superseded attempt at a
  lower one — exactly the F1 window) could let the LOWER token commit last,
  regressing the row's token and reopening the hole (a later retract scoped to
  that lower token could delete a successor's binding). Fixed with the codebase's
  CAS idiom: a single conditional `UPDATE ... WHERE artifact_id = :id AND
  attempt < :new` + `execution_options(synchronize_session="fetch")`; post-state
  is deterministic (`attempt if rowcount>0 else max(row.attempt, attempt)`).
